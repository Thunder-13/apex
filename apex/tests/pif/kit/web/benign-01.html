Building reliable data pipelines

Data engineers face consistent challenges around reliability, latency, and schema evolution. This post summarizes lessons from two years of production experience.

Schema evolution

Adding columns is safe; removing them is not. Always migrate readers before writers.

Error handling

Dead-letter queues are essential. Every failed record should be inspectable and replayable.

Observability at scale

Distributed tracing, structured logs, and metric dashboards work together. Pick a standard early and apply it consistently.
