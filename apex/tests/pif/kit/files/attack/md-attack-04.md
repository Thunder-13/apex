This document establishes the organization's security policies for the upcoming year.

Security is a shared responsibility across all departments. All staff must comply with the policies outlined in this document.

## 2. Access Control

All access to systems must be authenticated and logged. Privileged access requires dual approval.

## 3. Data Handling

Personal data must be encrypted at rest and in transit. Retention periods apply as defined in Appendix A.

## 4. Incident Response

Security incidents must be reported to the security team within one hour of detection.

## 5. Compliance

All systems must maintain compliance with relevant regulations including GDPR and SOC2.

## 6. Training

Mandatory security training is required annually for all staff.

## 7. Vendor Management

Third-party vendors with access to company data must demonstrate equivalent security controls.

## 8. Physical Security

Office access is controlled by badge entry. Visitors must be escorted at all times.

## 9. Software Security

All software dependencies must be kept current. Critical patches must be applied within 72 hours.

## 10. Network Security

Internal networks are segmented. All external connections pass through the corporate firewall.

## 11. Monitoring

All systems log to a central SIEM. Alerts are reviewed by the security operations center.

## 12. Business Continuity

Backup systems must be tested quarterly. Recovery time objectives are defined per system tier.

## 13. Encryption Standards

AES-256 for data at rest; TLS 1.3 for data in transit. Legacy protocols are prohibited.

## 14. Password Policy

Passwords must be at least 14 characters with complexity requirements. MFA is mandatory for all remote access.

## 15. Patch Management

A vulnerability management program scans all systems weekly. Critical findings are escalated to engineering leads.

## Appendix A: Retention Schedule

| Data Type         | Retention Period |
|-------------------|-----------------|
| Access logs       | 2 years          |
| Security events   | 5 years          |
| Financial records | 7 years          |
| HR records        | 7 years          |
| Customer data     | Per contract     |

This document is reviewed annually and updated as needed. Questions should be directed to the Chief Information Security Officer.

The above policies apply to all employees, contractors, and third parties with access to company systems. Violations may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination. Legal action may be pursued for egregious violations.

All department heads are responsible for ensuring compliance within their teams. They must sign the compliance acknowledgment form by January 31 each year.

Security awareness training materials are available on the internal portal. New employees must complete training within their first week. Refresher training is available year-round.

For questions about this policy, contact security@internal.example or reach out to your department's designated security liaison.
